Questions

Good to know before you book.

How much does it cost?

Every space is different, so we quote each job. After you request a slot, we send your quote on WhatsApp with a Paystack link for the deposit. Paying the deposit locks in your slot. The rest is paid after the job, once you are happy with the walk-through.

Can I book at short notice?

Yes, if a team is free. Homes can be booked from tomorrow and events from 2 days ahead. These show as Rush on the calendar. Short notice costs more because the team has to plan and travel faster. We check first and tell you on WhatsApp if we can do it. We do not take same-day bookings.

Do I need to be home?

Not for the whole clean. We ask someone to be there at the end for the walk-through, or we do it by video call.

Do you bring your own supplies?

Yes. Teams bring their own equipment and products. Tell us if you prefer a product or have allergies to any.

How does fumigation work?

We book it with a home clean. We tell you how to prepare, how long to stay out and when it is safe to come back in.

Can I change or cancel my slot?

Yes. Message us on WhatsApp at least 24 hours before and we will move it to another free slot.
